# Using Keepsake

Sign in to [Keepsake](https://ourkeepsake.vercel.app), open a new chat and
choose the mode in the header or under **Where it thinks** in the side pane.
Vault Mode keeps the prompt on your device. If the local model isn't available,
you can leave the message there or switch to Hybrid yourself.

## Read a cloud request before sending it

For example, suppose you enter `Draft a reply to sam@example.com about the
meeting.` If Hybrid needs the cloud, the preview shows `Draft a reply to
[email#1] about the meeting.` The card names the destination and lists the email
address as a replacement. Read the rest of the message too: a name or detail
in the surrounding prose may still identify someone.

Choose **Approve and send** to send the text shown, or **Keep it local** to
decline. When the rules find nothing to replace, Hybrid can use the cloud
without an extra tap. The badge below the reply identifies its source.

## Manage your chats

Open the side pane to revisit a conversation. Press and hold its title to
rename or delete it. **Delete on-device chats** clears all conversations in
this browser. Signing in on a different device does not restore them.

For device requirements and the shared demo login, see the [README](../README.md).
